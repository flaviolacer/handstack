# HandStack Roadmap

The implementation sequence follows the milestones in the master specification. `STATUS.md` is the durable execution ledger and records verified progress, commands, risks, and the immediate next step.

1. M0: repository, operational, documentation, governance, and extension foundations.
2. M1–M6: persistence, identity, providers, chat, gateway, budgets, and usage.
3. M7–M12: capabilities, agents, MCP, and plugin runtime/UI.
4. M13–M18: knowledge, governance, multi-agent execution, workflows, marketplace, and production hardening.

Milestones are verification boundaries, not release promises or dates. A milestone is complete only when its specification criteria and relevant quality gates pass. The project-wide completion marker is reserved for the fully integrated and audited specification.
