# HandStack Governance

HandStack uses a maintainer-led, transparent governance model during its pre-release phase.

Maintainers review changes, releases, security responses, and repository access. Routine decisions are made through reviewed pull requests. Durable or cross-cutting architectural decisions require an ADR. Changes that affect product requirements must update the stable requirements catalog and traceability matrix.

Consensus is preferred. When consensus cannot be reached, the maintainers responsible for the affected subsystem decide after documenting alternatives and consequences. A maintainer must disclose conflicts of interest and abstain when impartial review is not possible.

The governance model, maintainer roster, and decision process must be revisited before the first stable release. Community and Enterprise editions must use the same core; packaging and entitlements may differ without source forks.
