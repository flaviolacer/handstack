# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in HandStack welcoming and harassment-free for everyone, regardless of identity, background, experience, or ability.

## Expected behavior

Be respectful, address impact constructively, give actionable feedback, protect private information, and prioritize the safety of users and contributors. Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, doxxing, and sustained disruption are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when conduct threatens a safe and productive community. Report incidents privately using the contact described in `SECURITY.md`; never disclose another person's sensitive information in a public issue. Maintainers will acknowledge reports, investigate proportionately, avoid conflicts of interest, and communicate the outcome when appropriate.

This policy applies in project spaces and when someone publicly represents HandStack.
