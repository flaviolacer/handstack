# Configure versioned model pricing

## Purpose and expected outcome

Keep provider and model pricing tenant-scoped and historically reproducible by resolving the latest version effective at the time of usage.

## Prerequisites

- An organization with registered providers and model definitions.
- `models.manage` to register prices and `budget.manage` to reconcile usage.

## Steps

1. Register a `ProviderPricing` or `ModelPricing` record with an effective date and currency.
2. Set input, output, cached-input, image, audio and request prices using the documented units.
3. Add a new effective-dated version when a provider changes price; do not rewrite historical records.
4. Resolve pricing at the usage timestamp and pass the estimate into the normal budget settlement pipeline.

## Verify

Resolve before and after a price change and confirm each timestamp selects the correct version. Confirm that a tenant cannot resolve another tenant's price and that negative usage is rejected.

## Troubleshooting

- No effective price means no record exists on or before the requested timestamp.
- A duplicate-version error means the same tenant/resource/effective date already exists.
- A validation error means a price or usage dimension is negative or non-finite.

## Security, budget, and audit implications

Pricing is organization-scoped and contains no credentials. Estimates are inputs to budget reservation/settlement; the pricing catalog does not bypass authorization, budget or audit controls.

## Rollback

Register a new effective-dated price version. Preserve earlier records so historical reconciliation remains reproducible.
