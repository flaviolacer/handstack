# Manage service accounts

## Purpose and expected outcome

Create a tenant-scoped machine principal with explicit scopes without sharing a human password, session or login flow.

## Prerequisites

- An organization administrator with `identity.manage`.
- A secure secret manager for the one-time returned client secret.

## Steps

1. Issue a service account with a display name, unique least-privilege scopes and optional expiry.
2. Store the returned secret immediately; HandStack stores only its digest and never returns it again.
3. Authenticate workloads with the client ID and secret, then authorize the resulting `SERVICE_ACCOUNT` principal through the normal policy engine.
4. Rotate the secret during credential maintenance and revoke the account when the workload is retired.

## Verify

Confirm the machine principal is organization-scoped, the secret is absent from the stored account, the old secret fails after rotation, and tenant mismatch or revocation fails closed.

## Troubleshooting

- `INVALID_CREDENTIALS` means the client ID, organization or secret is wrong.
- `EXPIRED` means the account expiry has passed.
- `DISABLED` means the account was revoked or disabled and needs administrative review.

## Security, budget, and audit implications

Service accounts never use human login or local password flows. Their scopes remain subject to HandStack authorization, rate limits, budgets, audit and capability execution controls.

## Rollback

Revoke the account and issue a new least-privilege account if a secret is exposed. Do not reuse or restore a compromised secret.
